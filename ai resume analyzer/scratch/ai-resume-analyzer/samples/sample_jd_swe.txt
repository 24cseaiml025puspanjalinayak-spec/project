Job Title: Senior Backend / Cloud Software Engineer
Company: NextGen Systems Inc.
Location: Remote / San Francisco, CA

About the Role:
We are looking for an experienced Senior Backend Software Engineer to lead the architecture, design, and deployment of our next-generation cloud infrastructure and microservices platform.

Key Responsibilities:
• Design, build, and maintain mission-critical backend services and REST APIs with high availability, scalability, and sub-100ms response times.
• Architect scalable cloud infrastructure on AWS utilizing Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform.
• Optimize relational databases (PostgreSQL) and distributed caching systems (Redis) for high-concurrency workloads.
• Establish continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines with GitHub Actions.
• Collaborate in an Agile/Scrum cross-functional team, mentoring engineers and championing TDD and software engineering best practices.

Requirements & Qualifications:
• 5+ years of software engineering experience building scalable backend platforms.
• Strong proficiency in Python or Go.
• In-depth hands-on experience with FastAPI, Django, or comparable backend frameworks.
• Deep expertise in relational databases (PostgreSQL) and caching (Redis).
• Extensive cloud experience with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes (k8s), and Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform).
• Solid understanding of Microservices, System Design, REST API, and CI/CD pipelines.
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
